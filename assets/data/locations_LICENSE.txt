Offline location list (assets/data/locations.json)

Provincial capitals of Türkiye, cities of Europe and Northern Cyprus, and most
district centres of Türkiye: GeoNames (https://www.geonames.org), licensed
under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 License
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), obtained through the
geonamescache 3.0.2 and all-the-cities 3.1.0 packages.

Districts (ilçe) of Türkiye and the remaining district centres: Countries
States Cities Database (https://github.com/dr5hn/countries-states-cities-database),
licensed under the Open Database License (ODbL) v1.0
(https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/). Some of its records carry
names or points from GeoNames (CC BY 4.0), © OpenStreetMap contributors
(ODbL 1.0) and Wikidata (CC0).

Changes: places selected (all districts of Türkiye; European cities with about
50,000 or more inhabitants and capitals of countries and their first-level
regions; six towns of Northern Cyprus), central districts ("Merkez") merged
into the provincial capital, local or Turkish names shown, other names kept
for search only, coordinates rounded to four decimals.

The location list is a derivative database made available under the Open
Database License v1.0; the complete database is the file
assets/data/locations.json shipped with this app.
